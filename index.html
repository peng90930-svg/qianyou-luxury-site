    How buying from us works

    You see every bag in detail before anything is paid for or shipped.

    1. Tell us what you need

      Brands, models, colours, condition grades, whether you accept repaired pieces, quantity and target prices. Your own buying sheet is welcome.

    2. Receive a selection

      We send a list of matching pieces from stock or from our sourcing, with prices in euro.

    3. Review detailed photos

      For the pieces you shortlist, we send close-up photos of every mark, scuff and flaw, plus what accessories come with it.

    4. Confirm and ship

      Once you confirm the items and the order, we invoice, pack and ship to your address.
